Engineered for trust, safety, and accountability

Unlike opaque complaint boxes or unmoderated social walls, redFlag enforces strict data isolation and genuine privacy.

01

Substantiated Anonymity

When you select the anonymous protocol, your name, telephone, and email are never recorded in our database records. You receive a cryptographic tracking key that allows progress monitoring without ever establishing identity.

02

Restricted Evidence Handling

Supporting photographs and documents are securely encapsulated on private storage partitions. Media is never exposed through open public directories and is accessible strictly to authorized departmental reviewers.

03

Immutable Audit Trails

Every status transition from initial submission to final resolution generates a timestamped history entry. Staff cannot delete or silently suppress grievances without creating an auditable transition event.

Standard Operating Procedure

The redFlag Intake & Verification Protocol

Learn how incident reports progress from citizen submission to formal departmental inquiry and resolution.

1

Incident Lodging & Cryptographic ID Generation

The reporter completes the standardized filing form, selecting the appropriate incident category, specifying the exact occurrence timestamp, and entering location details. If the reporter chooses the anonymous preference, all contact fields are stripped on the server.

Result: An immutable reference code (e.g., RF-2026-9K8M2T) is issued immediately on the Acknowledgement screen.
2

Departmental Triage & Assessment

Authorized departmental officers access the restricted Review Queue to inspect incoming filings. Reports undergo preliminary validation to weed out spam, duplicates, or frivolous claims. Officers verify supporting documents or photographs attached to the file.

Status Transition: The report state changes from Submitted to Under Review. A timestamped audit entry is logged.
3

Formal Inquiry & Field Referral

Substantiated claims are forwarded to the relevant enforcement unit (child welfare officers, anti-corruption bureaus, cyber investigation cells, or municipal inspectors). Investigators append confidential internal progress notes to track ongoing interviews or evidence gathering.

Status Transition: The report state updates to Investigating.
4

Resolution & Public Accountability Update

Upon completion of field inquiries, the case is concluded. The reviewing officer records a formal outcome note explaining the enforcement action taken, statutory notice issued, or case referral. The reporter can view this outcome on the tracking portal using their reference code.

Status Transition: The report status updates to Resolved or Closed.

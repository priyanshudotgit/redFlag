Citizen Charter & Platform Usage Terms

User obligations, malicious reporting prohibitions, and operational disclaimers.

1. Solemnity & Factual Accuracy

By submitting an incident filing through redFlag, you declare that all statements, dates, locations, and descriptions provided represent truthful, accurate accounts to the best of your direct knowledge and recollection.

2. Severe Penalties for Malicious or False Reporting

Submitting intentionally fraudulent, fabricated, or malicious criminal accusations against individuals, public servants, or organizations is a severe statutory offense under penal law.

Frivolous filings divert vital investigative resources from authentic emergencies. Fraudulent submissions will be documented and referred for criminal prosecution.

3. Not a Live Emergency Dispatch Service

redFlag is an investigative civic intake tool. It does not replace immediate 911/112 police and medical dispatch systems. Submissions are reviewed during departmental triage windows and are not guaranteed immediate real-time response.

4. Intellectual Property & Uploaded Evidence

By uploading photographs, documents, or media files, you grant redFlag and verified investigatory authorities an irrevocable license to inspect, process, analyze, and retain the media for case adjudication.
